# Northwind Analytics Ltd. — Annual Report FY2025 (SYNTHETIC)

> SYNTHETIC DATA: fictional company, created for a RAG demo. Not real financial information.

## Letter to shareholders

FY2025 was the year Northwind Analytics completed its shift from project-based analytics services to a subscription cloud platform. Total revenue for the year reached USD 522.7 million, up from USD 431.0 million in FY2024, an increase of 21.3 percent. Cloud subscription revenue grew to USD 328.3 million and now represents 63 percent of total revenue.

Gross margin improved to 55.7 percent for the year, compared with 52.9 percent in FY2024, driven by higher utilisation of our shared data infrastructure and a lower mix of hardware pass-through costs.

## Strategy

Our strategy rests on three priorities. First, expand the cloud platform with industry-specific data models for retail, logistics and healthcare. Second, grow net revenue retention above 115 percent by selling additional modules to existing customers. Third, keep the services business stable and profitable while steering new customers to the platform.

We do not expect services revenue to grow materially in FY2026. Management views services as an onboarding channel for cloud customers rather than a growth engine.

## Operating expenses

Operating expenses rose to USD 177.7 million in FY2025. The increase came mainly from research and development headcount in the Hyderabad and Pune engineering centres and from a larger enterprise sales team in Europe. Research and development accounted for roughly 44 percent of operating expenses.

## Capital allocation

The company ended FY2025 with USD 212 million in cash and no long-term debt. The board approved a share buyback programme of up to USD 40 million over two years and does not intend to pay a dividend in FY2026.

## Outlook

For FY2026 management guides to revenue growth of 16 to 19 percent, with cloud revenue growth above 25 percent and an operating margin between 21 and 23 percent.
