# Northwind Analytics Ltd. — Risk Factors (SYNTHETIC)

> SYNTHETIC DATA: fictional company, created for a RAG demo. Not real disclosures.

## Customer concentration

Our ten largest customers accounted for 27 percent of revenue in FY2025. The loss of, or a significant reduction in spending by, any of these customers could materially affect revenue.

## Competition

We compete with large cloud providers that bundle analytics with storage and compute, and with specialised analytics vendors. Bundled offerings may force us to lower prices in the mid-market segment, which would reduce gross margin.

## Foreign exchange

A substantial portion of revenue is billed in euros and British pounds, while the majority of our costs are incurred in Indian rupees. Appreciation of the rupee against these currencies would reduce operating margin. Our hedging programme covers only part of this exposure.

## Data security and regulation

We process sensitive customer data. A security breach could result in liability, regulatory penalties under data-protection laws such as the GDPR and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, and loss of customer trust.

## Talent

Our growth depends on hiring and retaining engineers in a competitive market. Wage inflation in our engineering centres could increase operating expenses faster than revenue.
