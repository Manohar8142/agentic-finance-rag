## Prepared remarks — Chief Executive Officer

Thank you all for joining. Revenue for the second quarter of FY2026 was USD 155.3 million, up 20.9 percent year over year. Cloud revenue was USD 107.1 million, up 34.9 percent year over year, and crossed two thirds of total revenue for the first time.

We signed 41 new enterprise customers in the quarter, including our largest logistics contract to date. Net revenue retention for the cloud platform was 118 percent.

## Prepared remarks — Chief Financial Officer

Gross profit was USD 89.2 million, a gross margin of 57.4 percent, an improvement of about 2 points from a year ago. Operating income was USD 35.8 million, an operating margin of 23.1 percent. Net income was USD 26.7 million.

Operating expenses were USD 53.4 million. We slowed hiring in general and administrative functions while continuing to invest in research and development.

We are raising full-year FY2026 revenue growth guidance to 18 to 20 percent, from 16 to 19 percent previously, and we now expect operating margin at the top of our 21 to 23 percent range.

## Questions and answers

Analyst: What drove the margin improvement this quarter?

CFO: Two things. Infrastructure costs per customer fell after we consolidated data centres, and services, which carry lower margins, were a smaller share of revenue.

Analyst: Any change in the competitive environment?

CEO: Pricing pressure has increased in the mid-market, especially from larger cloud vendors bundling analytics with storage. We are responding with industry-specific models rather than price cuts.

Analyst: How exposed are you to foreign exchange?

CFO: About 38 percent of revenue is billed in euros and pounds while most of our engineering cost is in Indian rupees, so a stronger rupee would compress margins. We hedge roughly half of the next two quarters of exposure.
